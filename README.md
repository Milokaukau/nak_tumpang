# Nak Tumpang

An app that allows you to request for tumpang / fetch others.

## About

Nak Tumpang makes short-distance, informal ride-sharing simple, reliable, and community-driven. It helps people request or offer rides for errands, commutes, events, or one-off trips without the overhead of commercial ride-hailing.

- Problem it solves: informal coordination for rides is often ad-hoc, costly, or unreliable; Nak Tumpang provides a lightweight way to connect riders and drivers locally.
- Who it helps: commuters, students, event-goers, people without regular transport, and community members who want to offer or request lifts.
- Core value: fast, low-friction matching with clear pickup/dropoff details and basic safety/communication features.
- Benefits: lowers travel cost and wait time, strengthens local connections, reduces empty-car miles, and enables ad-hoc carpooling.
- Goals:
  - Short-term: let users post requests and accept offers with minimal setup.
  - Long-term: add trust signals (ratings, profiles), route-based matching, scheduling, and map/notification integrations while keeping the app lightweight.
